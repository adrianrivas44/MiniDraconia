# Rights and provenance review

**Status: public provenance inventory; not legal clearance.** The maintainer explicitly requested this public community publication, including the product soul and identity manual. That authorization is not a license from third parties, a legal opinion, or proof that every provenance question below is resolved. Please flag a specific concern through the repository's reporting channels; do not assume the engine's GPL applies to every asset.

## Different materials, different questions

- **Engine/code:** derived from Chaozhong Liu's DyberPet. Preserve the inherited GPL-3.0 license, copyright and source obligations. Do not relabel the derivative MIT merely because it is a new repository.
- **Character:** adult, fan-created reinterpretation inspired by Draco Malfoy. The franchise and trademarks are not owned by this project. A non-affiliation disclaimer is informative, not a grant of rights.
- **Character text/manual:** the project maintainer authorized preparing the product soul and visual guide for sharing. This does not authorize publishing a private agent profile, conversation history or secrets. The original manual describes private-use provenance; review its transition to public use explicitly rather than silently changing that statement.
- **Images/animation:** inventory per source and transformation. An image-generation provider label does not prove authorship of the reference image, permission for embedded official symbols, or rights in a derivative character. Review the actual assets.
- **Audio:** generated clips need provider/source provenance and redistribution review independently of listening approval. Listening approval is not a license. Pending clips must not be made approved by editing a flag.
- **Fonts/manual embedding:** check font embedding/redistribution notices separately. A claim in a manual that a font uses OFL does not replace the font's actual license/provenance.
- **Qt and dependencies:** include the actual applicable license and notice texts for redistributed libraries. A commercial-license reference file alone is not proof that open-source redistribution requirements have been satisfied.

Do not claim all project contents share one unrestricted license. Do not copy the private intake archives into Git. This document records review questions; it is not a new contributor agreement, a legal opinion or a change to existing rights.
